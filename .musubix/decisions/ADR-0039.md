# ADR-0039: Bundled toy 50-gene/5-pathway universe for enrichment, not a real pathway database

## Context / 背景
REQ-AGENOM-040 needs a deterministic, offline hypergeometric gene-set enrichment test with no network call and no external pathway database such as MSigDB or Reactome.

## Decision / 決定
Bundle a small fixed toy CSV universe (`GENE01`-`GENE50` across exactly 5 overlapping pathways) in `src/ai_genomics_scientist/data/sample_gene_sets.csv`, and compute `p_value` via `scipy.stats.hypergeom.sf` directly over that fixed universe, always returning the full 5-entry result list sorted by ascending `p_value` then `pathway`.

## Rejected alternatives / 却下した代替案
Shipping or downloading a real pathway database (e.g. a vendored MSigDB subset): rejected as disproportionate scope, a licensing concern, and a violation of the no-network-call/no-external-bioinformatics-library convention this skill must follow; the toy universe is instead explicitly documented as such, not presented as a biological validity claim.

## Consequences / 結果
Results are only meaningful relative to the bundled toy universe; any future requirement to support real gene sets needs a new requirement defining how a larger universe is supplied and validated.
